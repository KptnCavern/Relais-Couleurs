A MAGICAL PUZZLE

Let color guide your way

A relaxing puzzle game with no timer in its main campaign, where simple rules unfold into increasingly surprising routes.

◈

Pick your direction

Jump to the nearest compatible crystal in any of the four directions, then change color as you cross it.

✧

Unlock the way out

Visit checkpoints in order to unlock the exit. Watch out for detours and dead ends.

✺

Discover new powers

Renaissance brings visited crystals back to life; Rainbow accepts every color. More surprises will enrich the journey.
