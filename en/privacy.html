Privacy policy

This page describes the intended practices for Relais Couleurs (Android package: com.kc.relais), published under the KptnCorp name. It must be reviewed and updated before final publication and whenever advertising or purchases are activated.

1. Publisher and contact

Publisher: KptnCorp. Developer website: https://kptncorp.fr. For questions about personal data, use the developer contact details listed on the app’s Google Play page. A dedicated contact address must be added here before this page goes live.

2. Game progress and preferences

The game stores certain functional information locally on the device: level progress, earned stars, available hints, tutorials viewed and audio preferences. This website does not require an account.

3. Rewarded advertisements — planned

The game plans to offer optional rewarded video ads in exchange for hints. When enabled, Google AdMob and its partners may process advertising identifiers, technical data and information about ad interactions, subject to applicable consent choices and requirements. This website does not imply that rewarded ads are already live.

4. In-app purchases — planned

Optional features (ad removal and hint packs) are being considered. If introduced, payments will be handled by Google Play. This website does not process payments.

5. Consent and choices

Before showing ads requiring consent, the application must offer the required information and controls, including for users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Users will be able to revisit advertising choices through the mechanisms made available in the app.

6. Recipients and retention

Local progress data stays on the device, subject to Android operating-system backup mechanisms. If advertising or purchase services are enabled, their data processing and retention will also depend on the providers’ policies. Exact retention periods and any international transfers must be confirmed before publication.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection and withdrawal of consent. Contact the publisher using the Google Play developer contact details. You may also contact the relevant supervisory authority.

8. Website

This is a static website. This version has no form, user account, third-party analytics or advertising cookies. The web host may still keep technical access logs under its own policies.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated after confirming which features and services are actually used in the app.
